# Out-of-scope ideas (not built)

- **Quantities per assembly:** the manual has a *Quantity* column for each subassembly. It could be used to build bills of materials or maintenance kits (for example, how many 104.049 screws a full machine uses).
- **Diagram from the part card:** each part already stores its diagram image (`posicion_en_diagrama`). The app could show that image when you tap a part, but the photos would have to be published.
- **Finer modules:** `Other` covers the hood, steam and coffee outlets, drip tray, bean hoppers, electric box and water pump. If the owner or accounting prefer a different grouping, new modules can be added.
- **Shared data across devices:** today each browser has its own copy. A backend or shared database would remove the need to export and import.
- **Truck stock and replenishment:** these were in v0.1 and are out of scope for this phase.
